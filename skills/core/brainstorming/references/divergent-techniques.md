# Divergent Thinking Techniques — Reference Hub

Use this hub to choose a divergence technique before opening the detailed child reference.

## Technique Selection Guide

| Technique Family | Best For | Child Reference |
|------------------|----------|-----------------|
| SCAMPER, What-if, Analogy, First Principles | Incremental variation, assumption breaking, cross-domain transfer, and ground-up reframing | `divergent-techniques-classic.md` |
| Constraint Removal, Reverse Engineering, TRIZ | Working against strong constraints, goal-backward design, and contradiction resolution | `divergent-techniques-systems.md` |
| Lateral Thinking, Morphological Analysis, Brainwriting 6-3-5, Random Entry | Fixation breaking, combinatorial coverage, iterative idea building, and novelty rescue | `divergent-techniques-exploratory.md` |

Start with the technique family least natural to the topic.
That choice counteracts anchoring and usually opens a wider option space than the obvious first technique.
